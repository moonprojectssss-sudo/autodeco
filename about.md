# Auto Deco

Decorate your layouts and generate new ones, right inside the level editor.

## Auto Deco

Build a layout, tap the green **Deco** button next to Trash, pick a **theme** and a **mood**, and deco is placed for you:

- small pieces on top of your blocks and hanging underneath them,
- big pieces in the background,
- accents on the beat of your song (enter the BPM and the level speed).

**Themes:** Space, Slaughter, Neon, Forest, Cyber

**Moods:** Clean, Slaughterhouse, Limbo, Dreamy

**Density:** Low, Medium, High

You choose the objects. Select deco you like in the editor and save it to a slot (Edge, Hang, BG, Beat). Every theme remembers its own palette.

When it finishes, everything it placed stays selected. Recolor all of it at once with Edit Object, or press Trash to undo.

## Layout Generator

Tap the blue **Gen** button to build a fresh cube layout:

- **Difficulty:** Easy, Normal, Hard, Harder, Insane, Demon
- **Length:** about 15, 30, 60 or 90 seconds
- **Speed:** 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x
- **Seed:** reuse a seed to get the same layout again

Every layout is different: spikes, platforms, stairs, pads and rhythm patterns are mixed with a random motif each time. Gaps follow safe jump distances and scale with speed. Always playtest the result.

## Safe by design

Auto Deco only keeps decoration objects. Anything that is a block, a spike or a portal is removed again immediately, so decorating can never make your level harder or impossible.

## Good to know

- Works on Android and desktop, built for Geode 5.x.
- Beat sync assumes a constant speed and a song that starts with the level.
- Full guide, tips and troubleshooting are in the README on GitHub.

# v1.1.0
- New **Layout Generator** with Easy, Normal, Hard, Harder, Insane and Demon difficulties, four lengths, four speeds and seeds.
- Pattern library: single, double and triple spikes, platforms, stairs, spikes on platforms, pad jumps and double-single rhythms.
- Every layout gets a random motif, so no two layouts feel the same.
- Redesigned Auto Deco window with panels, mood descriptions, theme tips and a help button.
- The Deco and Gen buttons now sit at the top of the editor next to Trash.
- Auto Deco now uses the whole level when your selection only contains deco.
- All text, documentation and metadata are in English.
- New logo.

# v1.0.0
- First release: Deco button in the editor, five themes, four moods, beat sync and palettes saved from your selection.
